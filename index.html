Gopika Premsankar

Tenure-track Assistant Professor
Department of Information and Communications Engineering
School of Electrical Engineering, Aalto University

Before joining Aalto University as an assistant professor, I was an Academy of Finland postdoctoral researcher at the University of Helsinki, and a postdoctoral researcher at Ivey Business School in Canada funded by the Finnish Cultural Foundation. I completed my doctoral research in 2020 at the Department of Computer Science at Aalto University (supervisor: Prof. Mario Di Francesco). I have also spent time in research labs at VU Amsterdam, Duke University, Nokia Bell Labs and IBM Research Ireland.

My research focuses on improving the performance, reliability and sustainability of cloud and networked systems. Read more about our group's research here
